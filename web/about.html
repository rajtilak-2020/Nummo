About Nummo

Nummo is an autonomous personal finance ledger built for Android and Web. It requires no sign-ups, connects to zero remote servers, contains zero telemetry or advertising SDKs, and stores all records locally on your device.

Get on Google Play → Open Web App Privacy Policy

01. Philosophy & Why Nummo Exists

Most contemporary budget apps mandate remote account creation, scrape device SMS inboxes, request invasive permissions, and upload financial activity to multi-tenant cloud databases.

Nummo rejects this approach. It treats personal bookkeeping like a physical paper ledger or scientific calculator—a fast, reliable tool that opens instantly, works offline, and keeps your private financial records entirely on your device.

Core Guarantee: No backend servers, no user accounts, no external tracking. Your data never leaves your device.

03. Technical Architecture

  • Framework: Built with Flutter and Dart using sound null safety.
  • Local Persistence: Dual-write persistence with shared_preferences and flutter_secure_storage.
  • Document Synthesis: Client-side export formatting for PDF, Excel, and CSV with zero external services.
  • Zero Sockets: The application contains no background HTTP sync tasks or tracking SDKs.
