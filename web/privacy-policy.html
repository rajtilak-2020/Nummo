Privacy Policy & Security Specification

Nummo is an offline personal finance ledger engineered on a strict zero-ingestion premise: your financial transactions, balance sheets, categories, and cryptographic credentials reside exclusively in your physical device memory.

01. Core Architecture & Privacy Philosophy

Nummo ("the Software", "the App", or "the Service"), engineered and published by K Rajtilak, is an autonomous, standalone personal accounting ledger and mathematical budget tool.

Traditional personal finance applications frequently mandate remote account creation, scrape device SMS inboxes, harvest contact books, connect directly to open-banking APIs, or continuously upload ledgers to multi-tenant cloud databases. Nummo rejects this paradigm entirely. It operates as a closed-circuit, client-side offline appliance.

Architectural Guarantee: Nummo does not maintain backend servers, cloud databases, user authentication pipelines, or analytics endpoints. We do not possess the technical means to collect, view, decrypt, monetize, or transmit your financial ledger.

03. On-Device Storage & Cryptography

  • Operating System Sandboxing: On Android, data is persisted to the private application filesystem (/data/user/0/com.nummo.app/). Inter-process isolation enforced by the Linux kernel prevents other apps from reading or modifying this storage.
  • Cryptographic PIN Storage: If you configure a security PIN, it is salted with high-entropy random bytes and hashed via SHA-256 / PBKDF2 before persistence. The plaintext PIN is never stored.
  • OS Cloud Auto-Backup Exclusion: Nummo explicitly enforces android:allowBackup="false" and android:fullBackupContent="false" in its manifest, prohibiting unencrypted cloud backups.
  • On-Device Report Generation: Document exports (JSON, CSV, PDF, Excel) are synthesized 100% in-memory using client-side routines and written strictly via Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF).

Prohibited Permissions

Nummo omits all invasive permissions: READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, READ_CONTACTS, READ_CALL_LOG, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, and Advertising ID tracking.

05. Google Play Financial Disclosures

  • Non-Lending Declaration: Nummo is an offline bookkeeping and mathematical expense calculation utility. Nummo is not a lender, credit broker, loan facilitator, or financing platform. It does not issue, disburse, market, or broker loans.
  • Non-Banking Declaration: Nummo is not a bank, NBFC, depository institution, or payment processor. It does not hold custodial funds or process electronic fund transfers.
  • No Credit Scoring: Nummo does not calculate credit scores or transmit data to credit reporting agencies.

06. Statutory Frameworks

  • GDPR (EU & UK): Fully compliant with Data Minimization (Art. 5(1)(c)), Right to Erasure (Art. 17), and Data Portability (Art. 20).
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): Zero sale, sharing, or profiling of personal data.
  • DPDP Act 2023 (India): Full digital sovereignty. No user data is ingested by external servers.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): Zero data collection applies universally across all age groups.

07. Data Lifecycle & Erasure

  1. In-App Purge: Execute a complete, irreversible wipe of all records via Settings → Danger Zone → Reset All Local Data.
  2. OS Storage Reset: Clear data anytime via Android Settings → Apps → Nummo → Storage → Clear Data.
  3. Uninstall: Deleting the app instantly destroys the local sandbox and all stored records.
