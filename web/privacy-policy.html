Nummo: Expense Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

100% Offline & Privacy First

1. Overview & Privacy Pledge

Nummo ("we", "our", or "the App"), created by K Rajtilak, is designed from the ground up as a fully offline, private personal finance ledger.

We firmly believe that your financial information is strictly your own. We do not collect, track, store, transmit, or share any of your personal or financial data with external servers, third-party analytics providers, or advertisers.

2. Data Storage & Encryption

All data entered into Nummo—including transactions, notes, categories, budgets, and security preferences—is stored exclusively on your local device:

  • Local Persistence: Data is written to your device's isolated app sandbox using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences and SharedPreferences.
  • No Cloud Sync: The App contains zero backend servers, cloud databases, or telemetry tracking.
  • User Data Deletion: You can permanently wipe all stored data at any time from within the App via Settings → Reset All Data or by clearing app data in your device's Android system settings.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Nummo requests only the minimum hardware capabilities necessary to operate:

  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Used exclusively to unlock the app vault when you enable the optional App Lock feature. All biometric authentication is processed on-device by Android's native BiometricPrompt security subsystem. The App cannot and does not read, store, or transmit your biometric credentials or templates.
  • Storage & File Access: When exporting reports (PDF, CSV, Excel) or backing up/restoring JSON files, the App uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) system picker. The App only accesses files you explicitly pick or save.

4. Financial Services Policy Disclosures

In accordance with Google Play Financial Services policies:

  • Nummo is an offline personal budgeting tool and expense log utility.
  • Nummo is not a bank, loan provider, financial lender, credit broker, or payment processor.
  • Nummo does not provide loans, credit lines, money transfers, or banking services.
  • Nummo does not read your SMS messages or financial notifications. All transactions are created manually or imported from user-provided files.

5. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

The native Android App makes zero background network connections and integrates no third-party SDKs for analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising.

The only external network actions occur if you explicitly tap an external link (such as the developer website, GitHub repository, or this Privacy Policy) to open it in your device's default web browser.

6. Children's Privacy

Nummo does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with child privacy protection regulations (including COPPA and GDPR).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published to this URL (https://nummo.krajtilak.in/privacy_policy.html) and documented in the application changelog.
